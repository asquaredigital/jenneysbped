B.P.E.S

Bachelor of Physical Education and Sports

  • Duration: 3 Years
  • Eligibility: Passed +2 (10+2 Pattern), Govt. of Tamil Nadu or equivalent
  • Affiliated to: Tamil Nadu Physical Education and Sports University (TNPESU)
  • Gender: Male & Female
  • Intake: As per university norms

The B.P.E.S programme is designed to develop physical educators and sports professionals with strong theoretical and practical knowledge. The curriculum covers sports science, coaching, fitness training, and sports management.

B.P.Ed

Bachelor of Physical Education

  • Duration: 2 Years
  • Eligibility: Any UG / PG Degree from a recognized university
  • Affiliated to: Tamil Nadu Physical Education and Sports University (TNPESU)
  • Gender: Male & Female
  • Intake: As per university norms

The B.P.Ed programme prepares graduates to become qualified physical education teachers and sports coaches. Admissions are strictly made following the guidelines of TNPESU, ensuring high academic and professional standards.
